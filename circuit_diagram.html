⚠️ Critical: HC-SR04 Echo Pin Voltage Divider (Wire S12)

The HC-SR04 Echo pin outputs 5.0V. ESP32 GPIO pins are rated for 3.6V max. You MUST wire the resistor divider to prevent chip damage:

HC-SR04 Echo (5V) ────[ 1kΩ ]────┬────> ESP32 GPIO 18  (safe 3.3V)
                                  │
                           [ 2kΩ ]
                                  │
                                 GND

⚠️ Important: L298N ENA/ENB Jumper Caps (Wires S1 & S6)

The L298N ships with small black jumper caps on the ENA and ENB pins, shorting them to 5V (100% speed always ON). You must physically remove both jumper caps and connect ESP32 GPIO 13 → ENA and GPIO 25 → ENB for PWM speed control.
